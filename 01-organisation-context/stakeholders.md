# Stakeholder Register

| Stakeholder | Primary Interest | Security Responsibility | Example Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chief Executive Officer | Business continuity and reputation | Approves risk appetite and major risk decisions | Meeting minutes, risk approvals |
| Clinical Director | Safe and continuous patient care | Ensures clinical workflows protect patient information | Clinical procedures, access approvals |
| Compliance Officer | Legal and policy obligations | Maintains compliance programme and coordinates assessments | Policies, compliance reports |
| IT Manager | Reliable and secure systems | Implements technical controls and maintains systems | Configurations, tickets, logs |
| Operations Manager | Efficient service delivery | Maintains operational procedures and tracks remediation | Procedures, action trackers |
| Human Resources | Workforce lifecycle | Coordinates screening, onboarding and termination | HR records, termination checklists |
| Finance Lead | Revenue and payment processes | Protects payment and financial information | Reconciliations, access reviews |
| Clinicians | Patient care | Use systems appropriately and report incidents | Training records, acknowledgements |
| Medical Virtual Assistants | Patient administration | Follow approved access, communication and documentation processes | Training, workflow records |
| Managed IT Provider | Technical support | Performs contracted technical and security activities | Contract, reports, service tickets |
| Patients | Privacy and service availability | Receive notices and report concerns | Notices, complaint records |
| Vendors | Service delivery | Meet contractual security and notification requirements | Contracts, attestations |

## RACI Exercise

Assign who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted and Informed for:

- User onboarding : Human Resources
- User termination : Clinical Director
- Security-incident reporting : IT Manager
- Vendor onboarding : Clinical Director
- Risk acceptance : IT Manager
- Policy approval : Compliance Officer
- Backup testing : Operations Manager
