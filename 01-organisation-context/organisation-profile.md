# Organisation Profile

## Organisation Name

**Meridian Virtual Health**

## Status

Fictional organisation created for an educational healthcare GRC portfolio.

## Industry

Telehealth and remote healthcare services.

## Mission

To provide accessible, coordinated and patient-centred virtual healthcare while protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of health information.

## Services

- Remote medical consultations
- Patient registration and intake
- Appointment scheduling
- Electronic clinical documentation
- Prescription coordination
- Patient follow-up
- Medical billing support
- Remote administrative support
