# Business-Process Inventory

| Process | Trigger | Main Activities | Information Used | Systems | Process Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Patient registration | New patient request | Collect identity and contact details | Patient identity data | Intake form, EHR | Operations |
| Appointment scheduling | Booking request | Select provider, date and time | Identity and appointment data | Scheduler, EHR | Medical VA Lead |
| Telehealth consultation | Scheduled appointment | Verify patient, conduct consultation | Clinical and identity data | Telehealth platform, EHR | Clinical Director |
| Clinical documentation | Consultation completed | Record assessment and care plan | Clinical information | EHR | Treating Clinician |
| Prescription coordination | Provider instruction | Send or track prescription | Clinical and pharmacy data | EHR, pharmacy system | Clinician |
| Patient follow-up | Follow-up due | Contact patient and document outcome | Contact and clinical data | EHR, phone/email | Medical VA Lead |
| Billing | Service completed | Prepare and submit claim or invoice | Identity, service and payment data | Billing platform | Finance Lead |
| Workforce onboarding | New employee hired | Create accounts and assign access | Employee and access data | HR system, identity platform | HR |
| Workforce termination | Employment ends | Disable access and recover assets | Employee and account data | HR and IT systems | HR/IT |
| Vendor onboarding | New service required | Assess, approve and contract vendor | Vendor and security information | Contract repository | Compliance |
| Incident reporting | Suspected event | Record, triage, escalate and respond | Security and operational data | Ticketing system | IT Manager |

## Week 1 Review Questions

1. Which processes are essential for patient care?
2. Which processes handle the most sensitive information?
3. Which processes depend heavily on third parties?
4. Which failures could stop the organisation from operating?
5. Which processes need formal evidence of approval or review?
