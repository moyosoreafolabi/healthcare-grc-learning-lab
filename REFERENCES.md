## Referencing Standard

1. Prefer official framework or regulator sources.
2. Record the source title and publication date.
3. Link to the official source where permitted.
4. Distinguish direct requirements from personal interpretation.
5. Do not upload copyrighted textbooks, paid standards or examination questions.
